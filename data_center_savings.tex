Adjusted Savings Model – 97.75% Band Power on First Registered Tokens

New Cost Model

First occurrence* of any unique block → registered & archived at *97.75%** of normal token cost (2.25% immediate saving + full bit-perfect extractability).
All subsequent duplicates* of that same block → fully deduplicated (cost ≈ *0**).

This matches the core design:  
Everything new is archived and extractable. All redundancies are merely deduplicated.

Assumptions for the Tables Below

1 token ≈ 4 characters  
Base cost: $0.50 per million tokens  
Three realistic uniqueness rates for data-center / spam / log / tracking traffic:

| Scenario              | Unique Content | Duplicate Content |
|-----------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Moderate redundancy   | 10%            | 90%               |
| High redundancy       | 3%             | 97%               |
| Extreme (spam/USPS)   | 1%             | 99%               |

Summary of the Model

First registered blocks always cost 97.75% of normal (tiny overhead for perfect archival).
Every duplicate after that is essentially free.
At high redundancy rates (typical for spam links, shipping updates, repeated agent memory, log streams), savings approach 97–99%.
